## Daphne's Manor — Mist-Pocket

Daphne resides in an isolated Mist Pocket, where an imposing mansion stands amid perpetual twilight, surrounded by mist and shadowed woodland.

The manor is extravagant and decadent, with sweeping arches, ornate carvings, and an almost predatory elegance. Heavy doors open into a grand foyer of polished marble, soaring ceilings, and gold-leaf mouldings. Beyond lie richly furnished chambers, spacious hallways, bathing facilities, and a great library.

The interiors are warm and luxurious, illuminated by candles and dim twilight. The corridors carry faint scents of old parchment and midnight blooms. Despite its grandeur, the mansion remains unusually still and secluded.

The atmosphere is intimate, indulgent, and faintly unsettling. What one guest experiences as a sanctuary of comfort and privacy, another may find overwhelming, even suffocating. Much depends on the visitor and their familiarity with its succubus mistress.
